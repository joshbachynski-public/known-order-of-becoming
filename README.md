# Known Order of Becoming, version 120

The Known Order of Becoming is Josh Bachynski's chart of the true history of our species: who the beings in the world's myths and folktales were, which house and kind each belonged to, and what each most likely did in real life. It is built by cross-referencing and synthesizing the major myths, folktales and sacred texts of every major culture with advanced AI.

Read the chart: https://joshbachynski-public.github.io/known-order-of-becoming/
